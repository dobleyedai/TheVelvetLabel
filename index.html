About

The Velvet Label is an independent music project focused on creating and publishing original songs and music videos. The project combines songwriting, music production, visual creation, video editing, and digital publishing into a single original release workflow.

What We Do

This publishing workflow is used to manage original music video releases, including captions, metadata, and scheduled publishing for original content. All music, lyrics, audio, visuals, and videos published through this workflow are original and rights-controlled.

The Velvet Label

The Velvet Label is an original music publishing label focused on creating and releasing original songs and music videos.

This app is used to manage publishing workflows, captions, metadata, and scheduled posting for original music video content.

All music, lyrics, audio, visuals, and videos published through this workflow are original and rights-controlled.
